Questions people ask

What does “Buy window” mean?

The current month is one of the historically strongest months for sales in that category. It describes conditions, not a particular offer.

What does “Wait” mean?

A stronger sale period for the category starts within about two months. If the purchase is not urgent, holding off usually pays.

Why use the Consumer Price Index?

It measures prices people actually paid, across the whole country, every month, and it is public. That makes it a fair check on whether a category is really getting cheaper.

Does a falling index mean every product is cheaper?

No. It is an average. Individual products and stores vary, which is why each offer still needs checking against its own usual price.

Get told when a buy window opens

One short email when a category you care about moves into its strongest sale period, or when mortgage rates shift. No daily blasts. Stop any time.
