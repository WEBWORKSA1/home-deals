Events that move each year

  • Midsummer online sale events

    Large online retailers run multi-day events, with matching sales at competitors. Strong on smart home, small appliances and vacuums.

    Usually July

  • Spring home improvement events

    Home improvement stores discount mulch, plants, paint, tools and outdoor power equipment for the start of project season.

    Usually April

  • Fall online sale events

    A second round of multi-day online events ahead of the holidays, good for smart home devices and previous-generation robot vacuums.

    Usually October

  • Retailer anniversary and “way day” style sales

    Furniture and home retailers hold their own two-to-three-day events, usually in spring and again in fall.

    Dates vary

Questions people ask

What is the best month to buy furniture?

January and July, when showrooms clear stock before the February and August collections arrive.

When do appliances go on sale?

September and October for most major appliances, and May for refrigerators, with extra bundle offers on holiday weekends.

Is Black Friday really the cheapest time?

For small kitchen appliances, vacuums, smart home devices and tool kits, usually yes. For furniture, linens, holiday decor and flooring installation, other months are better.

How are the countdowns calculated?

From the calendar rules for each holiday, such as the third Monday in February for Presidents’ Day. Retailer-run events with dates that change each year are listed without a countdown.

Get told when a buy window opens

One short email when a category you care about moves into its strongest sale period, or when mortgage rates shift. No daily blasts. Stop any time.
