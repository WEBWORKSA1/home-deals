Where support goes

  1. Keeping the lights on

    Hosting, the data pipeline that refreshes prices, rates and recalls four times a day, and the tools behind it.

  2. More and better guides

    Research and writing time for buying guides, cost guides and new calculators.

  3. Promotion and reach

    Getting the calendar and Price Pulse in front of more households.

  4. People

    Paying contributors: deal scouts, writers, video makers and developers.

  5. Giveaways and prizes

    Funding prize rounds for readers.

Other ways to help

  • Submit a deal you have checked
  • Contribute your skills
  • Sponsor a section

Plain facts

Home.Deals is not a charity, and contributions are not tax-deductible. Supporters do not receive anything that affects what we publish. We never ask anyone to click on advertising.
