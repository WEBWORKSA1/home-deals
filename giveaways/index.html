Giveaways

Free prize rounds for readers. Each round is announced here with its prize and rules before entries open.

No round is open right now

A giveaway opens only when a prize is confirmed, with its value, dates and eligibility published on this page first. Leave your email and we will tell you when the next round opens.

Hear about the next round

How giveaways work here

  • Free to enter, always
  • Winners drawn at random
  • No ad clicks, purchases or donations required
  • Prize and rules published before entries open

Supply a prize

Brands can sponsor a round and are credited throughout.

Sponsorship options

Standing rules for every round

  1. No purchase necessary

    Entry is free. A purchase, a donation or a quote request does not improve your chances.

  2. Who can enter

    Legal residents of the 50 United States and the District of Columbia who are 18 or older at the time of entry. Void where prohibited or restricted by law. The operator, its contractors and their households are not eligible.

  3. How to enter

    Complete the entry form during the entry period. One entry per person per round. Automated or bulk entries are disqualified.

  4. No ad interaction

    You are never required or encouraged to click on, view or interact with advertising to enter or to win.

  5. Selecting winners

    Winners are drawn at random from all eligible entries within seven days of the closing date. Odds of winning depend on the number of eligible entries received.

  6. Notifying winners

    Winners are contacted at the email address on the entry. A winner who does not reply within ten days forfeits the prize and another entry is drawn.

  7. Prizes

    The prize and its approximate retail value are stated for each round before entries open. Prizes are not transferable and have no cash alternative unless stated. Taxes on a prize are the winner’s responsibility.

  8. Your information

    Entry details are used to run the giveaway and, only if you tick the box, to send you deal alerts. See the privacy policy.

  9. General

    The sponsor may cancel or change a round if it cannot run as planned. By entering you accept these rules and the sponsor’s decisions, which are final. Giveaways are not sponsored, endorsed or administered by any retailer or brand named on the site unless stated.
