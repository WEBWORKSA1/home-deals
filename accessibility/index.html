Accessibility

Our approach and how to report a problem.

Home.Deals is built to be usable by as many people as possible, and aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.2 at level AA.

What we do

  • Pages use semantic headings, landmarks and labelled form fields.
  • Everything can be reached and operated with a keyboard, with a visible focus indicator.
  • Text and controls meet contrast requirements in both light and dark themes.
  • Charts are accompanied by the figures in text or a table.
  • Motion is minimal and is switched off when your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Videos play only when you choose, in the YouTube player, which offers captions where the creator has provided them.

Known limits

Advertising and embedded video are supplied by third parties and may not meet the same standard. External websites we link to are outside our control.

Tell us about a problem

If something does not work with your browser or assistive technology, tell us through the contact page with the page address and what happened, and we will fix what we can.
