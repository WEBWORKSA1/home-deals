## Patterns across the sites

1. **Evidence beats percentages.** The trusted deal sites say why a price is good. Marketplace pages show a struck-through price and nothing else.
2. **Everyone asks for the email, few give a reason.** The best capture is an alert tied to something specific.
3. **Lead generation hides the consent.** The strongest quote flows show value first, such as a calculator or a cost table, and say plainly who will call.
4. **Method pages build trust.** "How we verify", "why trust us" and dated methodology notes appear on the sites people rely on.
5. **Nobody owns timing.** Calendars exist as one-off articles. No site read combines a live seasonal signal with public price data. That gap is the product.

- The 30-year fixed mortgage average was 7.11% for the week of 12 November 2026 (Freddie Mac via FRED), read by the site's own pipeline.
- Consumer Price Index, October 2026 (BLS): all items about +4.0% over the year, furniture and bedding about −1.3%, major appliances about −1.2%. The October 2025 index was never published, so the base month is estimated.
- 483 recalls were announced in the 300 days to 19 November 2026, of which the pipeline classified 120 as home products.
- Trademark search results showed "HOME DEALS" (U.S. registration 2967344) as cancelled in 2016 and "HOMEDEALS" (serial 86839220) as dead. This is not a legal clearance.
