How it works

  1. Tell us about the project

    Pick the service, answer two questions and add your ZIP code. It takes about a minute, and you see a ballpark price range straight away.

  2. We review every request

    A person reads it. We look for providers that cover your area and the kind of work you described.

  3. You hear back by the method you chose

    Where we have a provider to introduce, we tell you who they are before they contact you. Where we do not, we say so and point you to the best alternative.

Warning signs in any quote

  • A price that is only good today
  • A demand for a large cash deposit, or full payment before work starts
  • No written contract, or a contract with blank spaces
  • No licence number or proof of insurance when asked
  • Pressure to sign over an insurance claim or let the contractor negotiate it for you
  • A door-to-door offer after a storm using “leftover materials”

How to handle high-pressure sales

Compare like with like

Three quotes are only useful when they price the same job. Write a one-page scope, send the same page to every provider and compare materials, labour, allowances, permits and warranty line by line.

About quote requests

Is this free?

Yes. You never pay Home.Deals for a quote request. If we introduce you to a provider and you hire them, the provider may pay us a referral fee, which is how this section is funded.

Is Home.Deals a contractor?

No. Home.Deals is an independent information and introduction service. We do not perform, supervise or guarantee any work. Any contract is between you and the provider you choose, so check licences, insurance and references yourself.

Who will contact me?

Home.Deals, and only the providers we introduce for this request. We use the contact method you select. We do not sell contact lists.

Do I have to give a phone number?

No. A phone number is optional. Email is enough.

What if you have no provider in my area?

We tell you. Coverage is being built out region by region, and we would rather say so than pass your details to someone unsuitable. You still keep the ballpark range and the comparison checklist.

How accurate is the ballpark range?

It is a national range based on published cost guides, adjusted for your answers. Real quotes depend on your home, your region and site conditions. Use it to spot quotes that are far too high or suspiciously low.

Can I ask you to delete my request?

Yes. Use the contact link at the top of any page and we will remove it.
