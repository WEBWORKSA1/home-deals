Windows: costs and free quotes

Replacement windows and patio doors. What it costs, what changes the price and how to compare quotes.

Get quotes

$450 to $1,400 per window

Installed, depending on frame material and glass.

Window quotes are famous for high-pressure tactics: a large “list” price, a same-day discount and a manager’s call. The antidote is a per-window price in writing from at least three companies, for the same frame material, glass package and installation method.

Installation method is the hidden variable. An insert replacement keeps the existing frame and costs less. A full-frame replacement removes everything to the studs, costs more and is the right answer when the old frame is rotten.

What moves the price

  • Frame material
  • Glass package, double or triple pane and coatings
  • Insert versus full-frame installation
  • Window size, shape and floor level
  • Trim, rot repair and lead-safe work in older homes

Compare quotes on these points

  • Price per window, itemised
  • U-factor and solar heat gain ratings on the label
  • Insert or full-frame installation
  • Product and labour warranty, and whether it transfers
  • Who measures and who installs

Questions to ask every provider

  • Is this an insert or a full-frame replacement?
  • What happens if you find rot?
  • Is the quote good for 30 days?
  • Are you certified for lead-safe work on pre-1978 homes?

Questions people ask

How much do replacement windows save on energy?

Less than most sales pitches suggest. Savings depend on what you replace. Going from single-pane to efficient double-pane makes a visible difference. Replacing sound double-pane windows rarely pays back on energy alone.

Is a same-day discount real?

Treat any price that expires when the salesperson leaves as a sales tactic. A reputable company will hold a written quote for a reasonable time.

Can I cancel a contract signed at home?

Under the FTC Cooling-Off Rule, sales of $25 or more made at your home can generally be cancelled within three business days. The seller must give you a cancellation form.
