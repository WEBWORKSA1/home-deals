Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and your choices.

This policy explains what information Home.Deals handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies to this website only.

Summary

  • You can read everything on the site without creating an account or telling us who you are.
  • We receive personal details only when you send a form.
  • Advertising on the site is served by Google and its partners, who use cookies.
  • We do not sell contact lists.

Information you give us

When you send a form, such as a quote request, a deal alert sign-up, a giveaway entry, a deal submission, a support pledge or an application to work with us, we receive what you typed: typically your name, email address, ZIP code, an optional phone number and the details of your request. Forms are delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit, a form-processing service, which handles the submission on our behalf.

Information stored on your device

The site uses your browser’s local storage for convenience: your light or dark theme, notices you have dismissed, an unfinished quote request and your last calculator inputs. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google’s Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of some other vendors’ use of cookies at aboutads.info.
  • More detail: how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Where the law requires consent for advertising cookies, you are asked for it before personalised ads are shown.

Embedded video

Videos are provided by YouTube using its privacy-enhanced mode. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you press play. Once you do, YouTube’s own privacy policy applies.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses for security purposes, under its own privacy statement.

How we use what you send

  • To reply to you and carry out your request.
  • For quote requests, to introduce you to a provider for that request. We tell you who before they contact you, and we pass on only what is needed.
  • To send deal alerts or giveaway notices you asked for. Every message includes a way to stop.
  • To prevent abuse and keep basic records.

How long we keep it

Requests and correspondence are kept for up to 24 months, then deleted, unless you ask us to remove them sooner or the law requires longer.

Your choices and rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, withdraw consent to contact, or object to its use. Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA give you additional rights, including the right to complain to a regulator. To make a request, use the contact page. We do not sell personal information.

Children

The site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.
