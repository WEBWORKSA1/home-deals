Smart home and security deals

Thermostats, cameras, doorbells, locks and Wi-Fi gear. Here is when prices drop, how to judge an offer and where to look.

HD-07 Pilot Light

Smart home devices are priced by the companies that make the platforms, and they use hardware discounts to win households. The lowest prices cluster around the big midsummer and October online sale events and Black Friday week, when doorbells, cameras, speakers and thermostats often fall by a third or more.

Check the ongoing cost before the purchase price. Many cameras and doorbells need a subscription to store video, and a cheap device with a monthly plan can cost more over three years than a dearer one without it.

When prices drop

July and October online sale eventsDeep discounts on first-party devices
Black Friday and Cyber MondayBundles of cameras, hubs and sensors
Any timeUtility rebates on smart thermostats

What a good deal looks like

  • A first-party device at 30% to 50% off list during a sale event
  • A utility rebate on a smart thermostat stacked with a retailer sale
  • Multi-packs priced well under the single-unit price

Watch for

  • Subscriptions needed for recording or alerts
  • Devices near the end of security-update support
  • Hubs that lock you to one ecosystem

Questions people ask

When are smart home devices cheapest?

During the midsummer and October online sale events and Black Friday week.

Do I need a subscription for a video doorbell?

Often, yes, to save and review recordings. Add the plan cost over three years to the device price before comparing models.

Can I get a rebate on a smart thermostat?

Many utilities offer one. Search your ZIP code in the ENERGY STAR rebate finder, and check your utility’s marketplace, which sometimes applies the rebate at checkout.
