Patio, lawn and garden deals

Patio furniture, grills, mowers and garden equipment. Here is when prices drop, how to judge an offer and where to look.

HD-06 Cut Grass

Outdoor products are the clearest example of buying against the season. Stores need the floor space for holiday stock by October, so patio sets, grills and mowers drop from late August, and the markdowns deepen every few weeks until the stock is gone.

Spring sales look generous but mostly discount accessories and plants. For a large purchase such as a dining set, a gas grill or a riding mower, late summer and early fall win, as long as you accept a narrower choice of colours and sizes.

When prices drop

Late August to OctoberEnd-of-season clearance on patio furniture, grills and mowers
Memorial Day and the Fourth of JulyPromotions on grills and patio sets, not the lowest prices
AprilMulch, soil, plants and hand tools at spring events

What a good deal looks like

  • Patio sets and grills at 40% to 60% under spring prices during fall clearance
  • Floor models sold assembled at a further discount
  • Battery mowers and trimmers bundled with a second battery

Watch for

  • Cushions and covers sold separately
  • Clearance sold as-is with no returns
  • Recalled fire pits, heaters and umbrellas

Questions people ask

When is patio furniture cheapest?

From late August through October, when stores clear summer stock. Selection shrinks as prices fall, so decide what you want early and watch it.

When should I buy a grill?

September and October for the lowest price. Memorial Day and the Fourth of July bring promotions, but they sit above fall clearance prices.

Is it worth buying a mower at the end of the season?

Yes, if you have somewhere to store it. End-of-season mowers are discounted to clear space, and battery models keep their warranty.
