Where real discounts are published

These are the official sale pages and long-running deal communities worth checking, with what each one is good for. Links open the source’s own page.

  • Lowe’s: Deals, Deals of the Day and clearance

    One-day online deals, member-only offers, clearance, coupons and rebates in one place. Offers show their end dates.

    Retailer sale page

  • The Home Depot: Appliance sales

    Package savings on kitchen and laundry sets, plus floor care and home comfort. Bundle offers list an end date.

    Retailer sale page

  • IKEA: Offers hub

    Sort by member offers, limited-time deals and last-chance items. Membership is free and unlocks the member price.

    Retailer sale page

  • Wayfair: Furniture sale

    Was-and-now pricing with open-box options. Filter by price band and rating, and compare against recent selling prices.

    Retailer sale page

  • Ashley: Clearance furniture

    Price cuts across every room. Long financing offers run alongside, so read the terms.

    Retailer sale page

  • Ashley: Outlet

    Overstock and discontinued pieces at clearance prices with warranty cover.

    Retailer sale page

  • Bed Bath & Beyond: Deals

    Deals grouped by rugs, bedding, seating, bedroom, dining, patio and lighting, with a free-shipping threshold.

    Retailer sale page

  • Overstock: Limited-time deals

    A rolling carousel of limited-time deals and savings by category on furniture, rugs and patio.

    Retailer sale page

  • Target: Home deals

    Weekly home deals by department, a Deal of the Day and member offers, many of them ending on Saturdays.

    Retailer sale page

  • Best Buy: Smart home, security and Wi-Fi deals

    Filter by brand, price and what a device works with. Member tiers earn rewards on some brands.

    Retailer sale page

  • ENERGY STAR: Products and rebate finder

    Search rebates and special offers on certified products by ZIP code. Run by the U.S. EPA.

    Public program

  • Energy.gov: Home upgrades and rebates

    Explains state-run home energy rebates and links to each program. Federal tax credits ended after 2025.

    Public program

  • CPSC: Recalls and product safety warnings

    The official U.S. list of recalled consumer products, with remedies for each.

    Public program

  • Slickdeals: Furniture deals

    Community-posted deals ranked by votes, with filters for store, rating and price and deal alerts.

    Deal community

  • DealNews: Home and garden deals

    Staff-vetted deals that state how the price compares with other stores and past lows.

    Deal community

  • Brad’s Deals: Home deals

    Editor-written deals from hundreds of stores, each with a note on how the price was checked.

    Deal community

  • Ben’s Bargains: Home and garden deals

    Hand-picked deals with a running list of the day’s most popular offers.

    Deal community

  • Bob Vila: Latest deals

    Tool and home improvement deal round-ups grouped by retailer and sale event.

    Deal community

  • Hip2Save: Deals

    A deal blog with store pages, weekly ads and urgency tags such as “ends tonight”.

    Deal community

  • Groupon: Patio, lawn and garden

    Marketplace listings with percent-off badges. Compare the “original price” against other stores.

    Deal community

  • hotukdeals: Garden and DIY (United Kingdom)

    For UK readers: community deals with price comparisons and alerts.

    Deal community

  • OzBargain: Home and garden (Australia)

    For Australian readers: community-voted deals with expiry countdowns.

    Deal community
