Mattresses deals

Mattresses, bases, toppers and bed-in-a-box brands. Here is when prices drop, how to judge an offer and where to look.

HD-02 Sunday Morning

Mattress pricing is built around four long weekends: Presidents’ Day, Memorial Day, Labor Day and Black Friday. Outside those windows most brands still run a promotion, but the holiday offers tend to add the extras that carry real value, such as a free base, pillows or a longer trial.

Comparison shopping is deliberately hard. Many stores sell the same mattress under an exclusive model name, so match on construction instead: height, coil count, foam layers and firmness. For online brands, the trial period and return cost matter as much as the price.

When prices drop

Presidents’ Day (February)First big event of the year, strong on last year’s models
Memorial Day and Labor DayThe two widest sales, with bundles on bases and bedding
Black Friday weekOnline brands post their lowest advertised prices

What a good deal looks like

  • A queen priced at least 25% under its typical selling price, or a bundle worth that much
  • A sleep trial of 90 nights or longer with free or low-cost returns
  • A written warranty of 10 years or more that covers sagging at a stated depth

Watch for

  • Exclusive model names that block price matching
  • Trial periods that require a 30-night minimum before a return
  • Restocking or pickup fees on returns
  • Free gifts priced into an inflated list price

Questions people ask

What is the best month to buy a mattress?

February, May, September and November. The holiday weekends in those months are when both stores and online brands compete hardest, and when bundles are most generous.

Can I negotiate a mattress price?

In a store, usually. Margins are wide, so ask for a price match, free delivery, removal of the old mattress or an upgraded base. Online prices are fixed, but chat agents can often apply a current code.

Is a mattress recall check worth doing?

Yes. Mattresses sold online are recalled regularly for failing the federal flammability standard. Check the Recall Radar before buying an unfamiliar brand.
