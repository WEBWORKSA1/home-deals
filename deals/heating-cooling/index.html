Heating and cooling deals

Air conditioners, heaters, fans, purifiers and HVAC. Here is when prices drop, how to judge an offer and where to look.

HD-11 Thermostat

Buy heating in spring and cooling in fall. Portable and window air conditioners are cleared from late August, space heaters from March, and both cost the most during the first hot or cold week of the season, when stock is short.

For a full system replacement the same logic applies to labour. HVAC contractors are busiest in midsummer and midwinter. Schedule a replacement for early spring or fall and you gain time to compare quotes, which is where most of the saving comes from.

When prices drop

September and OctoberAir conditioners and fans on clearance, and installers available
March and AprilSpace heaters on clearance, and off-season HVAC pricing
Any timeUtility rebates on heat pumps and smart thermostats

What a good deal looks like

  • End-of-season units at 30% to 50% off
  • Three written quotes for the same equipment size and efficiency rating
  • A utility rebate confirmed before you sign

Watch for

  • Emergency replacements bought in a heatwave
  • Oversized equipment quoted without a load calculation
  • Recalled space heaters and dehumidifiers

Questions people ask

When is the best time to replace an HVAC system?

Early spring or fall, when contractors are less busy and you can compare quotes without an emergency.

Are there still tax credits for heat pumps?

The federal energy efficient home improvement credit ended for equipment placed in service after December 31, 2025. Utility and state rebates may still apply, so check by ZIP code.

When are air conditioners cheapest?

From late August through winter. Prices peak during the first heatwave of the year.
