Bedding and bath deals

Sheets, towels, comforters and bathroom basics. Here is when prices drop, how to judge an offer and where to look.

HD-08 Fresh Linen

January “white sales” are one of the oldest traditions in retail, and they still work: sheets, towels and comforters are discounted across department stores and home retailers for most of the month. August brings a second round aimed at students, with twin and twin XL sizes cheapest.

Thread count is a poor guide to quality. Fibre and weave tell you more, and a 300-count long-staple cotton sheet will outlast an 800-count blend. Compare the same fibre, the same weave and the same size.

When prices drop

JanuaryWhite sales on sheets, towels and comforters
AugustBack-to-college bedding and storage
Black Friday weekSome down comforters and towel sets, though January is usually lower

What a good deal looks like

  • Sheet sets and towels at 40% or more under regular price during white sales
  • Last season’s colours on clearance
  • Multi-buy offers on towels you were going to replace anyway

Watch for

  • Inflated thread counts on blended fabrics
  • Sets missing pillowcases or with shallow pockets
  • Weighted and heated blankets with open recalls

Questions people ask

What is a white sale?

A January sales tradition on household linens that dates back to the 1800s. Retailers still discount sheets, towels and bedding through the month.

Does thread count matter?

Only up to a point. Fibre quality and weave matter more. Above roughly 400, higher counts are often achieved with thinner multi-ply yarns that do not improve the sheet.

When are towels cheapest?

January, with a second dip in August during back-to-college promotions.
