Flooring and paint deals

Hardwood, vinyl plank, carpet, tile and paint. Here is when prices drop, how to judge an offer and where to look.

HD-10 Oak Plank

Flooring is cheapest when installers are quiet. Late December through January is the slow season, so both materials and installation are easier to negotiate. A second window opens in late spring, before summer remodelling peaks.

Paint works differently. Manufacturers run mail-in or instant rebates around Memorial Day, the Fourth of July and Labor Day, and those rebates on premium lines usually beat any everyday price.

When prices drop

December and JanuarySlow season for installers, so prices are negotiable
Memorial Day, Fourth of July, Labor DayPaint and stain rebates
Any timeRemnants and discontinued lots for small rooms

What a good deal looks like

  • Installed price per square foot quoted in writing, including underlayment, trim and removal
  • Discontinued lots large enough for the room plus 10% waste
  • Paint rebates of $10 or more per gallon on premium lines

Watch for

  • Free installation offers that raise the material price
  • Quotes that leave out subfloor repair, transitions and furniture moving
  • Too little overage for cuts and future repairs

Questions people ask

When is flooring cheapest?

Late December and January, when installers have fewer jobs. Late spring is the second-best window.

How much extra flooring should I buy?

About 10% for straight layouts and 15% for diagonal or herringbone patterns. The Flooring calculator adds this for you.

When does paint go on sale?

Around Memorial Day, the Fourth of July and Labor Day, when manufacturers offer rebates.
