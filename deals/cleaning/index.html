Vacuums and floor care deals

Stick, robot and upright vacuums, mops and carpet cleaners. Here is when prices drop, how to judge an offer and where to look.

HD-12 Soap Bubble

Vacuum makers tend to launch new models in early summer, which is why April and May bring the spring-cleaning discounts on outgoing ones. Black Friday week is the other low point, especially for robot vacuums and cordless sticks.

Robot vacuums are sold in long model ladders where a few letters separate a machine with a self-emptying dock from one without. Read the dock, mop and navigation features, not the series name.

When prices drop

April and MayOutgoing models discounted for spring cleaning
Black Friday weekRobot vacuums and cordless sticks at yearly lows
July and October online eventsLightning deals on previous-generation robots

What a good deal looks like

  • A previous-generation flagship at 40% or more off launch price
  • A cordless stick bundled with a second battery
  • Manufacturer-refurbished units with a one-year warranty

Watch for

  • Near-identical model numbers with missing docks or mops
  • Replacement bags, filters and pads that cost more than expected
  • Recalled cordless batteries

Questions people ask

When are vacuums cheapest?

April, May and Black Friday week.

Is last year’s robot vacuum worth buying?

Usually. Navigation and suction improve slowly, and the previous generation often sells for far less than the new one.

What does a robot vacuum cost to run?

Budget for bags, filters, brushes and mop pads. The Energy cost calculator covers the electricity, which is small.
