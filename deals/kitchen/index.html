Kitchen and small appliances deals

Cookware, air fryers, mixers, coffee makers and tableware. Here is when prices drop, how to judge an offer and where to look.

HD-04 Copper Pot

Small appliances and cookware are gift categories, so prices fall hardest when retailers compete for gift spending: Black Friday week, the December run-up, and the big midsummer and October online sale events.

These are also the products most often sold in slightly different versions for different stores. A stand mixer with a different bowl or a cookware set with one fewer pan can make a price look lower than it is. Compare by what is in the box.

When prices drop

Black Friday through mid-DecemberLowest advertised prices on mixers, air fryers and cookware sets
July online sale eventsShort lightning-style deals on small appliances
May and JuneWedding and graduation promotions on cookware and tableware

What a good deal looks like

  • A price at or below the item’s previous holiday low
  • Cookware sets priced under the sum of the pieces you would buy alone
  • Refurbished units sold by the manufacturer with a one-year warranty

Watch for

  • Store-specific versions with fewer accessories
  • Nonstick sets padded with utensils to raise the piece count
  • Recalled pressure cookers and air fryers resold on marketplaces

Questions people ask

When is cookware cheapest?

Black Friday week and early December, with a smaller dip during wedding season in late spring.

Is a refurbished small appliance a good deal?

Factory-refurbished units from the manufacturer usually are, because they carry a warranty. Marketplace “renewed” listings vary, so read who the seller is.

How do I compare cookware sets?

Count the pans, not the pieces. Lids and utensils are counted as pieces. Divide the price by the number of actual pots and pans you will use.
