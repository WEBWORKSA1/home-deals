Tools and hardware deals

Power tools, hand tools, storage and workshop gear. Here is when prices drop, how to judge an offer and where to look.

HD-05 Safety Vest

Tool deals follow gifting and project seasons. Father’s Day in June and the stretch from Black Friday to Christmas bring the combo-kit promotions, while home improvement stores run a spring event in April aimed at the start of project season.

The pattern to know is the battery platform. Brands discount kits with batteries heavily and bare tools lightly, and they run “buy a starter kit, get a free tool” offers that beat any straight percentage. Buy into one platform and add bare tools later.

When prices drop

Father’s Day (June)Combo kits and free-tool offers
Black Friday through ChristmasThe year’s lowest kit prices and gift sets
AprilSpring events at home improvement stores

What a good deal looks like

  • A starter kit with two batteries and a charger near the price of the bare tools
  • A free bare tool with a battery kit purchase
  • Hand-tool and mechanics sets at half the regular price during holiday events

Watch for

  • Kits bundled with small 1.5 Ah or 2.0 Ah batteries
  • Brushed-motor versions sold beside brushless ones at a similar price
  • Special-buy model numbers that exist only for the promotion

Questions people ask

When do power tools go on sale?

Around Father’s Day and from Black Friday to Christmas. Home improvement stores also run spring events in April.

Should I buy a combo kit or bare tools?

Start with a kit, because the batteries and charger are where the cost sits. After that, bare tools on the same battery platform are the cheaper way to expand.

Are store-brand tools worth it?

For occasional use, usually. Check the warranty length and whether the battery platform will still be sold in a few years.
