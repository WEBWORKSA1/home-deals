Decor, rugs and lighting deals

Rugs, lamps, mirrors, curtains and seasonal decor. Here is when prices drop, how to judge an offer and where to look.

HD-09 Plum Velvet

Decor follows the same two-season rhythm as furniture, with clearance in January and July. Rugs are close to permanently discounted online, so the real signal is a price well under the level the rug has held for the past few months.

Seasonal decor is the easy win. Holiday items fall by half or more in the days after the holiday, and artificial trees, lights and outdoor displays bought on December 26 are the cheapest they will be all year.

When prices drop

January and JulyClearance before new collections
The week after any holidaySeasonal decor at 50% to 75% off
Black Friday weekRugs and lighting at the year’s lows

What a good deal looks like

  • A rug at 60% or more under its list price and below its recent selling price
  • After-holiday decor at half price or better
  • Lighting bought as open-box with all parts present

Watch for

  • Rug sizes quoted loosely, such as a 5x7 that measures 4'11" x 6'6"
  • Rugs with open flammability recalls
  • Light fixtures without a safety certification mark

Questions people ask

When do rugs go on sale?

Rugs are discounted most of the year online. The lowest prices usually land in January, July and Black Friday week.

When should I buy holiday decorations?

Right after the holiday. Stock is limited, but prices fall by half or more within days.

How do I check a lamp or fixture is safe?

Look for a recognised testing mark such as UL or ETL on the product and check the Recall Radar for the brand.
