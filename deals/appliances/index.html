Major appliances deals

Refrigerators, ranges, dishwashers, washers and dryers. Here is when prices drop, how to judge an offer and where to look.

HD-03 Brushed Steel

Most major appliances are refreshed in the fall. Manufacturers release new ranges, dishwashers, washers and dryers around September and October, and retailers discount the outgoing models to clear them. Refrigerators are the exception: new models arrive in spring, so May is their month.

The best total price usually comes from stacking. Holiday events add bundle savings for buying two or more pieces from one brand, retailers price-match each other, and utility rebates for efficient models can still apply even though the federal energy tax credits ended on December 31, 2025.

When prices drop

September and OctoberOutgoing models cleared as new ones arrive
MayRefrigerators, when the new model year lands
Holiday weekends and Black FridayBrand bundles and free delivery or installation

What a good deal looks like

  • An outgoing model at 25% to 40% under its launch price
  • Bundle savings on two or more pieces, plus free delivery, hookup and haul-away
  • Open-box or scratch-and-dent units with the full manufacturer warranty

Watch for

  • Delivery, installation parts and haul-away billed separately
  • Extended warranties sold at checkout
  • Derivative model numbers made for one retailer, which defeat price matching
  • Rebates that need a mail-in form within 30 days

Questions people ask

When do appliances go on sale?

The steadiest discounts arrive in September and October as new models replace old ones, with refrigerators following the same pattern in May. Presidents’ Day, Memorial Day, the Fourth of July, Labor Day and Black Friday add bundle offers.

Is scratch-and-dent worth buying?

If the damage is cosmetic and on a side you will not see, it is one of the best values in the category. Confirm the manufacturer warranty still applies and inspect the unit before delivery.

Are there still rebates for efficient appliances?

Federal tax credits for home energy upgrades ended for purchases after December 31, 2025. Many utilities and some state programs still offer rebates. Search by ZIP code in the ENERGY STAR rebate finder before you buy.
