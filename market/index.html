Questions people ask

What does the meter measure?

National conditions for buyers, from 0 to 100. It combines the supply of new homes, the pace of house price growth and where mortgage rates sit in their 52-week range.

Does a high score mean I should buy?

No. It says national conditions lean towards buyers. Your decision depends on your local market, your finances and how long you plan to stay.

Why new-home supply?

It is published monthly by the Census Bureau and HUD and is in the public domain. It moves with builder incentives and price cuts, which are a direct form of “home deal”.
