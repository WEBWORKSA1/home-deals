Questions people ask

Where do these rates come from?

They are Freddie Mac’s weekly national averages from its Primary Mortgage Market Survey, published each Thursday and retrieved from FRED at the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis.

Will I get this rate?

Not necessarily. The survey is an average for borrowers with strong credit and a 20% down payment. Your rate depends on credit score, down payment, loan type, points and lender.

How often is this page updated?

The site checks for new data four times a day. The survey itself is weekly.

Should I wait for rates to fall?

Nobody can forecast rates reliably. A more useful question is whether the payment works for you today, and what a refinance would need to save to be worth doing later. The refinance calculator answers the second part.

Home.Deals is not a lender or broker and does not offer loans. Figures are national averages for information only.
