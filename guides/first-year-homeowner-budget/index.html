A first-year budget for a new homeowner

What to set aside for maintenance, which big-ticket items to plan for, and a seasonal checklist that prevents the expensive repairs.

Home money, 5 minute read

In short

  • Set aside 1% to 4% of the home’s value each year for maintenance and repairs.
  • Know the age of the roof, water heater and HVAC, because they fail on a schedule.
  • Small seasonal jobs prevent most large bills.

The mortgage payment is the predictable part of owning a home. The unpredictable part is everything attached to it, and the first year is when most new owners discover what the previous owner postponed.

How much to set aside

A long-standing rule of thumb is 1% to 4% of the home’s value per year for maintenance and repairs, with newer homes at the low end and older homes at the high end. On a $400,000 house that is roughly $330 to $1,330 a month. You will not spend it evenly. Some years cost little and one year replaces a roof.

Find the ages that matter

Your inspection report or the equipment labels will give you install dates. Typical service lives:

ItemTypical lifeTypical replacement cost
Tank water heater8 to 12 years$1,300 to $3,200
Central air conditioner12 to 17 years$4,500 to $9,500
Furnace15 to 20 years$3,200 to $7,800
Asphalt shingle roof20 to 30 years$5,500 to $16,000
Refrigerator10 to 15 years$900 to $3,000
Dishwasher9 to 12 years$500 to $1,400
Washer or dryer10 to 13 years$600 to $1,500 each

Anything within three years of the end of its range belongs in your plan. Collecting quotes before it fails is the single best way to avoid emergency pricing.

Do the cheap jobs on schedule

  • Spring. Clean gutters, check the roof from the ground, service the air conditioner, test the sump pump.
  • Summer. Seal gaps around windows and doors, check the deck and exterior paint.
  • Fall. Service the furnace, drain outdoor taps, clean the dryer vent, sweep the chimney.
  • Winter. Flush the water heater, test smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, check for ice dams.
  • Monthly. Change or check the HVAC filter.

Buy the first-year list at the right time

New owners buy more furniture, appliances and tools in twelve months than in the following ten years. Spread those purchases across the sale calendar and the saving is typically larger than any single negotiation.
