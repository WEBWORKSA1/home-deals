How to buy a major appliance for less

Model changeovers, bundle rebates, open-box stock and delivery terms: the levers that set the real price of a refrigerator, range or washer.

Buying guides, 6 minute read

In short

  • Outgoing models are cleared in September and October, refrigerators in May.
  • Bundle offers, price matching and utility rebates stack.
  • Delivery, installation parts and haul-away belong in the comparison.

Appliance prices move more than most people expect. The same dishwasher can sell for 30% less in October than in March, and the gap between the advertised price and the delivered, connected price can be several hundred dollars. Here is how to control both.

Buy the outgoing model

New ranges, dishwashers, washers and dryers reach stores in September and October, and new refrigerators in May. Retailers discount the previous model to clear it. The differences are often cosmetic, so the outgoing model at 25% to 40% under its launch price is the standard good deal in this category.

Price Pulse currently shows major appliance prices about 1.2% lower than a year ago compared with a year ago, based on the Consumer Price Index. Check the latest reading before you decide whether to wait.

Stack the savings

  1. Holiday bundle offers. Buying two or more pieces from one brand often adds a tiered rebate.
  2. Price matching. Large appliance retailers generally match each other on identical model numbers. Bring a link.
  3. Utility rebates. Search your ZIP code in the ENERGY STAR rebate finder. The federal tax credits ended after December 31, 2025, but utility and state programs continue.
  4. Card and portal offers. Activate them before you check out.

Consider open-box and scratch-and-dent

A dent on the side of a refrigerator that will sit between cabinets costs nothing in use. Ask whether the full manufacturer warranty applies, inspect the unit in person and confirm the return policy. Discounts of 20% to 50% are common.

Compare the delivered price

  • Delivery and whether it includes bringing the unit inside
  • Installation and required parts: hoses, cords, gas connectors, brackets
  • Haul-away of the old appliance
  • Whether doors need removing to get it through yours

Decide on the warranty at home

Extended warranties are sold at the moment you are least able to evaluate them. Check what your credit card adds, read the manufacturer warranty and decide beforehand. If you want cover, you can usually buy it within 30 days.

Repair or replace?

If a repair costs more than half the price of a comparable new appliance and the unit is past the middle of its expected life, replacement usually makes sense. The Repair or replace calculator applies that rule to your numbers and adds the energy savings of a newer model.
