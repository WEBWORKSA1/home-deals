How this calculator works

  • Running cost is watts × hours ÷ 1,000 × your price per kWh.
  • For the comparison, each model’s yearly kWh is multiplied by your rate and by the years you keep it.
  • Payback is the price difference divided by the yearly saving.

Results are estimates from the figures you enter. They are not financial or professional advice.

Questions people ask

Where do I find kWh per year?

On the yellow EnergyGuide label for most major appliances, or on the ENERGY STAR product listing.

Does this include gas?

No. It covers electricity. For gas appliances, use the therms per year on the label and your gas rate in place of kWh.
