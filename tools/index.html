Calculators

Eight calculators for the decisions that cost the most around a home.

  • Deal Score checker

    Find the real discount after coupons, cashback, delivery and tax, measured against what the item usually sells for.

  • Unit price comparison

    Compare up to three sizes or bundles by cost per unit, square foot, sheet or load.

  • Mortgage payment calculator

    Monthly payment with principal, interest, property tax, insurance and HOA, plus what a rate change does to it.

  • Refinance break-even calculator

    How many months until a refinance pays for its closing costs, and the total interest on both loans.

  • Energy cost calculator

    What an appliance costs to run, and how long an efficient model takes to repay its higher price.

  • Repair or replace calculator

    Apply the 50% rule and expected lifespan to a repair quote, with energy savings included.

  • Paint calculator

    Gallons and cost for a room, allowing for doors, windows, coats and the ceiling.

  • Flooring calculator

    Square footage with waste, boxes to order and the installed cost.

Calculators run in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent to us.
