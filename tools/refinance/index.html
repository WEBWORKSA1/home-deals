How this calculator works

  • Both payments are principal and interest only, so tax and insurance cancel out.
  • Break-even months are closing costs divided by the monthly saving.
  • Total interest is compared over the full life of each loan, which shows the effect of restarting the term.

Results are estimates from the figures you enter. They are not financial or professional advice.

Questions people ask

What is a good break-even?

Shorter than the time you expect to keep the loan. Under three years is comfortable for most owners.

Why can a lower payment cost more overall?

Because a new 30-year loan spreads the balance over more years. Compare total interest, or choose a term close to the years you have left.
