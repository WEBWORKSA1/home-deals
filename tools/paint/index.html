How this calculator works

  • Wall area is the room’s perimeter multiplied by its height.
  • Each door removes about 21 square feet and each window about 15.
  • Area × coats ÷ coverage gives gallons, rounded up to the next quart.

Results are estimates from the figures you enter. They are not financial or professional advice.

Questions people ask

Do I need primer?

For new drywall, patched areas, stains or a strong colour change, yes. Count primer as a separate coat.

When is paint cheapest?

Around Memorial Day, the Fourth of July and Labor Day, when manufacturers run rebates.
