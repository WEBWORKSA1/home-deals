How this calculator works

  • Each price is divided by its quantity to give a cost per unit.
  • The lowest unit price wins, and the others show how much more they cost.
  • Use the same unit for every option: all in ounces, all in square feet or all in pieces.

Results are estimates from the figures you enter. They are not financial or professional advice.

Questions people ask

Is the bigger pack always cheaper?

No. Promotions on mid-size packs often beat bulk sizes, which is why the unit price is worth a few seconds.

How do I compare cookware or tool sets?

Count only the pieces you would buy on their own. Lids, utensils and bits inflate the piece count.
